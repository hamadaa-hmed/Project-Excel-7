# 🛒 Superstore Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **Excel-based Sales Analysis project** built using the Superstore dataset.

The project focuses on analyzing sales performance from different business dimensions and turning raw sales data into meaningful insights that can support better business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to analyze:

- Sales performance
- Product performance
- Customer performance
- Geographic performance
- Time-based sales trends
- Profitability and business performance

The analysis helps identify strong and weak areas and provides a clearer view of overall business performance.

---

## 📊 Key Analysis Areas

### 💰 Sales Analysis

Analyze sales across different dimensions to understand overall performance and identify the areas generating the highest revenue.

### 📦 Product Analysis

Evaluate product performance and identify products that contribute strongly to sales and products with weaker performance.

### 👥 Customer Analysis

Analyze customer-level performance to identify important customers and understand their contribution to overall sales.

### 🌎 Geographic Analysis

Compare business performance across different locations and identify high-performing geographic areas.

### 📅 Time Analysis

Analyze sales across different time periods to identify trends, increases, decreases, and changes in performance over time.

### 📈 Profitability Analysis

Evaluate profit alongside sales to understand whether high sales are also generating strong profitability.
